Pop-Up Tales 📖

An app by Oxtail Games

Pop-up storybooks for your iPhone. Open the book and every page rises into a little paper scene, with tabs to pull and pieces to tap. Three stories with Pip the fox: The Little Lost Star, The Runaway Kite and Pip's Snowy Day.

How to read

  • Turn your phone sideways: the book reads in landscape.
  • Tap the cover to open the book, or drag the pull tab under it. Pinch with two fingers to close it again.
  • Swipe left or right, or tap the arrows beside the book, to turn the page.
  • Pull the paper tabs on each page to make things move, and tap the pieces to hear them.
  • While the book is closed, swipe the cover or tap the arrows beside it to choose another story.
  • On iPhone Duo, the book follows the hinge: open the phone to open the book, and fold it to close it.
