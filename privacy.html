Pop-Up Tales

Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 25, 2026

Pop-Up Tales is an app by Oxtail Games. This policy explains what the app does with your information. The short version: nothing leaves your device.

What we collect

Nothing. Pop-Up Tales does not collect, transmit, sell or share any personal data. It has no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking, and it never connects to the internet.

What stays on your device

The app remembers which story you chose last, so the same book is waiting on the shelf the next time you open it. This stays on your device and is deleted when you delete the app.

The hinge

On iPhone Duo, the app reads the angle of the hinge to open and close the book as you open and fold the phone. It uses this only while the app is open, and never stores or sends it anywhere.

Children

Because the app collects no data from anyone, it collects no data from children either.

Changes

If this ever changes, we will update this page, and the date above, before releasing the version of the app that changes it.
